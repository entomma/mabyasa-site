How it works

Mabyasa teaches Kapampangan through an adventure, not a flashcard deck. Meet villagers, learn their words, then use those words to win battles.

1

Meet and learn

Talk to characters in the world. Words are introduced naturally in conversation, from greetings up to full sentences and sayings.

2

Practice in battle

Answer correctly to attack. Words you master become combat cards, so what you know makes you stronger.

3

Build your Word Bank

Every word you meet is saved in your Word Bank with its meaning, how it's built and your progress.

Mayap a abak
